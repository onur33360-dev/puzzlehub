# PuzzleHub Monetization

## Philosophy

Monetization must never reduce player enjoyment.

Players should feel rewarded, not forced.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Revenue Sources

-   Premium Subscription
-   Rewarded Ads
-   Interstitial Ads (limited)
-   Diamond Packs
-   Cosmetic Purchases

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Premium

Premium removes ads and unlocks quality-of-life features.

Never create pay-to-win mechanics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Rewarded Ads

Allowed for:

-   Extra rewards
-   Continue options
-   Bonus diamonds

Always optional.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Interstitial Ads

Show only at natural breaks.

Never interrupt gameplay.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Economy

Currency:

-   Diamonds

Diamonds may be earned through:

-   Daily rewards
-   Achievements
-   Rewarded ads
-   Purchases

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Pricing Rules

Keep pricing simple.

Avoid confusing bundles.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## User Trust

Never use fake countdowns.

Never hide subscription cancellation.

Never mislead players.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Analytics

Measure:

-   Conversion rate
-   Ad completion
-   Retention
-   ARPDAU
-   Premium adoption

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Rule

Long-term trust generates more revenue than aggressive monetization.
